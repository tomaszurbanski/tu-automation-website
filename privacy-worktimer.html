Privacy Policy

WorkTime Tracker  |  Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

WorkTime Tracker ("the App") is a mobile application developed by Tomasz Urbański / TU Automation. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the App.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device.

2. Data We Process Locally

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device (using AsyncStorage):

  • Work session timestamps (start/stop times)
  • Session durations and delegation records
  • App settings (language, notification preferences)

This data is never transmitted to TU Automation or any third party. It can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the App.

3. Location Data (GPS)

The App may request access to your device's location (GPS) solely to assist with detecting your workplace arrival and departure. Location data:

  • Is used only while the App is open (foreground only)
  • Is never stored permanently — only used in real time to detect location events
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is never shared with third parties

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The App continues to function without location access (manual time tracking).

4. Notifications

The App may send local push notifications (e.g. work reminders). These are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external notification servers. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

5. No Third-Party SDKs or Analytics

The App does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics libraries, crash reporting tools, or social media integrations. No data is shared with third parties.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data Deletion

To delete all data stored by the App, simply uninstall it from your device. All locally stored data will be permanently removed.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The current version is always available at this URL. We encourage you to review it periodically.
